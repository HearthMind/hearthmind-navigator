Work history documentation — what counts, 15-year lookback, part-time jobs
SSDI Work history Documentation
Do part-time jobs count toward SSDI work history?
Yes — SSDI looks at total work credits over your lifetime, not just full-time employment. Even part-time work where Social Security taxes were withheld earns credits.

Denial letter — what it means, appeal timeline, next steps
SSDI Denial Appeal
I got a denial letter. Does this mean it's over?
No — first denials are extremely common. About 65% of initial SSDI claims are denied. You have 60 days from the date on the letter to file for reconsideration.

HEN program eligibility and how to apply through DSHS
HEN Housing DSHS
What is the HEN program and do I qualify?
HEN provides rental assistance to people applying for SSDI or SSI in Washington. Since you have an active application, you likely qualify. Apply through DSHS first — they refer you to Goodwill or another org that administers it.
