Work history documentation — what counts, 15-year lookback, part-time jobs
SSDI Work history Documentation
Do part-time jobs count toward SSDI work history?
Yes — SSDI looks at total work credits over your lifetime, not just full-time employment. Even part-time work where Social Security taxes were withheld earns credits. You need 40 credits total, 20 of which must be from the last 10 years.
What if I can't remember all my jobs from 15 years ago?
SSA can pull your earnings record — you don't have to remember everything perfectly. Request your Social Security Statement at ssa.gov/myaccount to see what they already have on file.

Denial letter — what it means and how to respond, appeal timeline
SSDI Denial Appeal
I got a denial letter. Does this mean it's over?
No — first denials are extremely common. About 65% of initial SSDI claims are denied. You have 60 days from the date on the letter to file for reconsideration, which is just the next step in the process.

HEN program eligibility and how to apply through DSHS
HEN Housing DSHS
What is the HEN program and do I qualify?
HEN — Housing and Essential Needs — provides rental assistance to people applying for SSDI or SSI in Washington. Since you have an active SSDI application, you likely qualify. You apply through DSHS, who then refers you to a community org like Goodwill that actually administers it.
